## IT-Support-Ticket-Analysis

**Tools used:** Power BI, Excel

**What I did:**
Analyzed 8,469 number of IT support tickets to identify trends in ticket volume, resolution time and common issue categories.

**Key Insights:**
- 8,469 total tickets analyzed, with an average resolution time of 7.58 hours.
- High-priority tickets take longer to solve than Low-priority tickets.
- Tickets volumes are evenly split across Open/Closed/Pending.
- Roughly 49% of resolved tickets had a resolution time logged earlier than first response time.

**Dashboard Includes**
- Ticket volume by status
- Avg resolution time by priority (Hours)
- Avg resolution time by channel (Hours)
